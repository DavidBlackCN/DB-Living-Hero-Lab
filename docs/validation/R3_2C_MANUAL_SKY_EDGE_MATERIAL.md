# R3.2c Registered sky-edge material

Status: implemented; awaiting human visual acceptance. The review target was clarified as the upper-right leaf cluster and the pale distant canopy/building tops below the spires.

## Diagnosis and correction

The open sky in this area already had near-full alpha in the R3.2b matte. Further additive sky-alpha painting made dark cuts in leaves and holes in the distant tree canopy. The conspicuous contrast comes mainly from pale foreground canopy/roof pixels against the deep Night sky. Those pixels must remain foreground. The rejected alpha-paint trials were discarded.

A hand-authored, 1672×941 [edge tone asset](../../public/assets/hero/sky/sky-edge-tone.png), regenerated by [paint_sky_edge_tone.py](../../scripts/paint_sky_edge_tone.py), shades only the selected upper-right foreground. Its polygon patches are softly feathered and clipped by `1 - sky-mask alpha`, keeping open sky untouched. The existing auto matte and small R3.2b additive override remain the sky-alpha foundation. Runtime applies the material after sky compositing with a weight derived from the existing Sky Night interpolation. It fades smoothly through Dusk/Night and Night/Dawn without changing `skyFor` or lighting curves. No new color threshold, dilation, or automatic hole heuristic was added.

This is a **local foreground edge-tone correction, not a claim that the alpha matte was rematted**. The four Sky RGB plates, their shared alpha, Base art, and global Lighting/Timeline/Blink/Leaves/Breathing behavior are unchanged. The compositing shader and asset registration changed solely to apply this material.
