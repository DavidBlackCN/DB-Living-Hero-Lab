# R5 Lighting Detail / Material Response

## Scope and reference

R4/R4.1b Character Motion Core was accepted and frozen before this work. R5 adds face, hair, and iris material response in the existing Lit shader. Base Albedo, Normal v3, Blink sprites, motion masks/parameters, Leaves, Sky RGB/matte, `lightingFor`, `skyFor`, and the time controller are unchanged.

The [Kumeng scene shader](https://github.com/buger404/KumengScreen/blob/main/lib/scene-shader.ts), [stylized lighting](https://github.com/buger404/KumengScreen/blob/main/lib/stylized-lighting.ts), and [daylight model](https://github.com/buger404/KumengScreen/blob/main/lib/daylight.ts) were inspected alongside live four-phase Lit previews. The useful direction was a readable face light plane and a localized, directional hair highlight whose importance recedes at Night. Its broader grading and post effects were not copied into this frozen lighting baseline.

## Implementation

- [`material-mask.png`](../../public/assets/hero/material/material-mask.png) is a registered 1672×941 RGBA texture, generated by [`paint_material_mask.py`](../../scripts/paint_material_mask.py). Red marks face/ear skin, green the top hair mass, blue the two irises. The mask uses source-space outlines plus local pigment guards to spare the bangs, hat, rose, white shirt, and background.
- The material mask follows the existing Breath/Head/Hair-deformed Albedo and Normal sample UV. The independent `Lighting Detail on/off` switch permits an exact previous-Lit comparison. Detail only runs in Lit; Base and Normal inspection views are unaffected.
- Face: a small warm skin reflectance adjustment, a four-neighbor mask-aware filtered Normal with strengthened XY volume, and a soft directional painted band. The frozen Normal asset is not modified. The face still follows the existing key direction and intensity.
- Hair: a curved, narrowing ribbon across the crown, broken up by source hair texture and driven by key direction/intensity. It is local rather than a screen-wide shine; its day visibility falls nearly to zero at Night.
- Iris: two small registered reflections with a key and weak ambient component. The reflection is disabled during the integrated closed Blink; it uses the same deformed UV as the eye.

## Six visual rounds

1. Face/skin channel and four-phase A/B; verified locality and no shirt or stone change.
2. First directional crown ribbon; it was too faint in the full frame.
3. Stronger face/hair response and initial iris reflection; Dusk crown became too broad and oily, while the glint was almost hidden on the dark pupil.
4. Reduced ribbon width/energy and protected the painted hair structure.
5. Narrowed the ribbon further and moved the iris reflection into the visible iris mask. Four full-composition 22-second previews checked all motion systems together.
6. Strengthened face Normal XY response modestly to make the face plane more apparent without lifting Noon whites or flattening Night. Repeated all static and 22-second full-composition captures. This is the final candidate.

## Validation and limits

- `python scripts/validate_lighting_detail.py`: passed. Mask registration, Detail OFF baseline, locality, Noon face/crown response, Night sheen suppression, and closed-Blink reflection behavior checked. Noon crown mean channel difference: 3.61/255; face: 1.75/255; Night crown: 0.06/255.
- `python scripts/validate_time_controller.py`, `python scripts/validate_blink_normal.py`, `python scripts/validate_hair_motion.py`, `python scripts/validate_breathing.py docs/validation/r5-breathing-tmp --strength 1.0`: passed. Frozen pixel comparisons disable R5 detail where appropriate.
- `python scripts/audit_lighting_24h.py docs/validation/r5-lighting-detail/24h-audit`: passed at 15-minute intervals. Noon artwork luminance 0.22107; 12:45 ties that peak. Evening face minimum 0.01836 remains above 22:00 face 0.01803. No lighting or timeline curve changed.
- `pnpm build` and `pnpm typecheck`: passed.
- The 72 sampled CPU render submissions during four 22-second previews had 0.3 ms median, with three spikes above 5 ms (37.4 ms maximum). This is a browser CPU submission observation, not a GPU timing claim. Motion previews showed no sustained stutter.

The fixed illustration and frozen Normal map limit how sculptural the face can become compared with Kumeng. The already accepted small top-leaf Sky edge residual and slight image-left clothing pull under stronger R4 head motion remain outside R5. R5 is ready for human visual acceptance; no later stage was started.
